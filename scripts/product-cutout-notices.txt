The model runs only in a worker in the administrator's browser.
The original product photo is not sent to a third-party inference API.
New cutouts have a paired uncut, optimized backup at <photo-path>.original.
Backups are not included in the public catalog's image list. The existing
products bucket remains public; backups have the same access as product photos.
The photo editor retains the original on cancellation, unsupported browsers,
model loading failure, timeout, or invalid masks. Existing photos are unchanged.
